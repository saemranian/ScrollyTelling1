# **App Name**: NarrativeFlow

## Core Features:

- Scroll-Controlled Video & Narrative Flow: Advance the stop-motion video segments and associated story points in precise sync with user scroll, creating an immersive, interactive narrative experience.
- Synchronized Dynamic Text & Titles: Display narrative text and expressive titles that smoothly appear, change, or fade in response to the user's progress through the story.
- Interactive Progress Bar: A subtle, non-intrusive visual indicator that intuitively shows the user's current position and overall progression within the narrative timeline.
- Generative Story Description Tool: An AI-powered tool to suggest or refine descriptive text for each story segment, assisting content creators in crafting compelling and fitting narratives.
- Story Content Uploader & Editor: An intuitive interface for content creators to upload stop-motion video segments, input corresponding text, and define their synchronization points within the story.

## Style Guidelines:

- The visual design emphasizes a 'warm earthy' color palette with a 'clean, minimalist' approach, evoking comfort and focus. The primary color, a rich yet muted brown (#A36629), provides a grounded, inviting feel, ideal for a narrative journey. It pairs seamlessly with a subtly warm off-white background (#F8F5F2) for maximum readability and visual serenity. For accent, a deep, desaturated olive (#5C552D) is chosen to introduce a natural contrast without overpowering the primary palette, adding depth and complementing the organic theme.
- A single typeface, 'PT Sans' (sans-serif), is selected for its humanist qualities, balancing modern cleanliness with a touch of warmth and personality. This versatile font will be used for both headlines and body text, ensuring legibility and maintaining a consistent, minimalist aesthetic across the interactive narrative.
- Icons will be kept minimal, clean, and line-based, focusing on subtle functionality like scroll indicators or navigation cues, aligning with the overall minimalist design. They should be discreet and not detract from the visual narrative.
- The layout is designed to be highly immersive and full-screen, prioritizing the stop-motion video and narrative text. Elements are placed with ample white space, ensuring a 'clean' and 'minimalist' appearance that guides the user's eye fluidly through the story as they scroll. Text overlays will be strategically positioned to enhance the visual narrative without obscuring critical video content.
- Animations are deliberately smooth and controlled by the scroll input, creating a seamless and organic progression through the story. Transitions for text and titles will be fluid fades or subtle glides, maintaining a soft, cinematic feel that complements the stop-motion video without jarring movements, embodying a 'clean' and 'interactive' experience.
